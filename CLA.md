## How to sign

breakdown uses [CLA Assistant](https://github.com/contributor-assistant/github-action)
to record signatures. The first time you open a pull request, a bot will
comment asking you to reply with a fixed phrase confirming you've read and
agree to this Agreement. That comment — your GitHub username, the text of
the phrase, and a timestamp — is your signature, and it's stored in our
signatures repository. You only need to sign once; the bot recognizes you on
every later pull request.

If this Agreement is ever revised in a way that changes what you're agreeing
to, we'll publish the new version at a new path and ask returning
contributors to sign again — your existing signature stays bound to the text
you actually signed.
